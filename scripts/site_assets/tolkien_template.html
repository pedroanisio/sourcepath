Geography is a derived view. Positions come from __METHOD__ over per-group mean chunk embeddings; the projection preserves neighbourhoods, not global distances, so the compass and any sense of scale are decorative tradition, not measurement.

What each mark traverses:

  • Relief — elevation is chunk density on the projected plane (Gaussian-smoothed, gamma-mapped); peaks mean many parsed chunks, sea means none.
  • Roads — the __ROADS__ strongest of __GEDGES__ inter-group import edges from the mechanical L1 graph (__DROPPED__ chart-less edges dropped; intra-group edges are cohesion, not coupling, and are rolled away).
  • Crossed swords — the top __NSTRESS__ routes by import-weight × semantic distance: strongly coupled yet semantically far apart.
  • Wyrms — settlements inside an import cycle of the L1 graph.
  • Frontiers — nearest-settlement realm boundaries on a half-resolution grid; realms are top-level directories.
  • Decorative only — parchment, border, cartouche, compass, sea-serpent, and the hand-wobble of every line.
